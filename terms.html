Terms, Refund & Dispute Policy

Short mein: Vajra ek verification aur facilitation service hai. Hum maal ke seller nahi hain. Token aur 90% payment seedha seller ko jata hai. Agreed limit se zyada quality issue ki zimmedari seller ki hai, jo sauda chithi mein likhit hoti hai.

1. Kaun is par lagu hota hai

  • Ye terms un sellers aur buyers par lagu hain jo apne vyapar ke liye ("business use") Vajra ki service lete hain. Consumers ke liye ye platform nahi hai.
  • Vajra se sampark, sample mangwana ya deal shuru karna in terms ko maanna mana jayega.

2. Vajra ki bhumika

  • Vajra surplus textile lots ke liye seller aur buyer ko jodta hai, live verification karta hai aur packing, seal aur delivery mein madad karta hai.
  • Vajra maal ka seller, owner ya insurer nahi hai. Maal ki asli quality aur kanooni zimmedari seller ki hai.
  • Vajra buyer ke paise hold nahi karta aur payment ka agent nahi hai. Sample fee ke alawa token aur baaki payment seedha seller ke account mein jati hai.

3. Verification aur "Verified by Vajra" seal

  • Vajra live video par maal, panna measurement aur burn test dikhata hai aur jo issue dikhe wo buyer ko saaf batata hai. Ye reasonable care ke saath ki gayi drishy (visual) jaanch hai, laboratory test nahi.
  • "Verified by Vajra" seal ka matlab sirf itna hai ki parcel Vajra ki maujoodgi mein, live video par pack hua. Ye maal ki quality ki guarantee ya warranty nahi hai.
  • Chhupi hui kami ya transit mein hone wala nuksan verification ke bahar hai.
  • Quantity ±2% ho sakti hai, jaisa lot par likha ho.

4. Sample fee

  • Sample ke liye ₹100 (pan-India) sample fee lagti hai.
  • Sample dispatch hone ke baad fee wapas nahi hoti. Hum sample bhej hi nahi paaye to fee wapas hogi.
  • Sample pasand na aaye to deal cancel ho jati hai, buyer par koi aur charge nahi lagta.
  • Sample design ya fabric ki copy banane ke liye nahi hai. Sample ke saath buyer ka naam, GST aur mobile number liya jata hai.

5. Token (10%) aur baaki payment (90%)

  • Sample pasand aane par buyer 10% token seller ke account mein deta hai. Ye payment seller ko jata hai, Vajra ko nahi.
  • Token ka refund sauda chithi ki shartein tay karti hain. Vajra refund karwane mein madad karta hai, par khud bharpai nahi karta.
  • Parcel ki LR (hold par) banne aur buyer ke transport office mein verify karne ke baad buyer baaki 90% seller ke account mein deta hai.
  • 90% payment ke baad seller NOC release karta hai aur uski copy Vajra buyer ko bhejta hai.

6. Seller ki warranties

  • Maal seller ka apna hai, uspar koi kanooni rok ya vivaad nahi hai, aur wo bechne ka adhikari hai.
  • Seller lagu kanoon ke hisaab se GST invoice aur e-way bill (jahan zaroori ho) jaari karega.
  • Seller ne jo jaankari di (quantity, panna, fabric, rate) wo sahi hai.

7. Quality claim

  • Delivered maal mein verification report se alag issue nikle aur wo sauda chithi mein likhi limit (3-5% of lot) se zyada ho, to seller zimmedar hoga.
  • Claim ke liye: seal sahi haalat mein ho, maal kholte waqt ki unboxing video ho, aur delivery ke 48 ghante ke andar Vajra ko likhit ya WhatsApp par claim batayi jaye.
  • Verification report mein pehle se bataye gaye issues par claim nahi banta.
  • Transit ka nuksan (bheegna, kat-fat) transport/insurance ke niyam se chalega.

8. Refund aur cancellation

  • Sample ke baad deal cancel: koi token nahi, sirf sample fee (section 4).
  • Token ke baad cancel: sauda chithi ki shartein. Seller sample ya report se alag maal bheje to token wapas karna seller ki zimmedari hai.
  • 90% payment ke baad: claim section 7 ke hisaab se.

9. Fees aur commission

  • Vajra ka service fee/commission har deal se pehle likhit mein bataya jata hai. Bina bataye koi charge nahi.
  • Vajra ke dwara introduce ki gayi party ke saath, introduction ki date se 12 mahine tak hone wali deals par sauda chithi mein likhi commission Vajra ko deni hogi, chahe deal Vajra ke bina hi kyun na ho. Ye ek payment ki shart hai.
  • Fee par lagu GST alag se lagega.

10. Recording aur data

  • Live video calls, packing aur verification record ho sakti hain. Seller aur buyer iske liye sahmati dete hain. Ye record dispute mein saboot ke roop mein use hoga.
  • Naam, GST, mobile jaisi jaankari sirf deal, verification aur dispute ke liye use hoti hai, aur kanoon ke bina kisi teesre ko nahi di jati.

11. Dispute kaise solve hoga

  • Step 1 (sampark): contact@vajrab2b.com ya WhatsApp +91 95104 01971 par lot ID, photo/video aur samasya bhejein.
  • Step 2 (sulah): Vajra dono pakshon se baat karke record (video, report, sauda chithi) dekhta hai aur sulah karwane ki koshish karta hai. Vajra faisla dene wala nyayadhish nahi hai.
  • Step 3 (arbitration): Sulah na hone par vivaad Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 ke tahat ek sole arbitrator ke paas jayega. Seat Surat hogi aur bhasha Hindi/English/Gujarati ho sakti hai. Arbitrator ko dono paksh milkar chunenge.

  • Ye seema Vajra ke dhokhe (fraud) ya jaanbujh kar ki gayi galti par lagu nahi hoti.
  • Vajra ke control ke bahar ki ghatnayein (dange, natural aapda, transport hadtal, sarkari rok) ke karan deri ya nuksan ke liye Vajra zimmedar nahi hai.
