Hold-LR Process: Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

Hum maal ko pehle dekhte hain, buyer ko sach batate hain, phir deal aage badhti hai. Payment ke baad hi LR/NOC release hota hai.

1

Live godown verification

Vajra godown mein jaakar live video call par poora maal, panna measurement aur burn test sahi roshni mein dikhata hai. Jo bhi kami hai, saaf bataayi jaati hai.

2

Sample (₹100 fee)

Buyer ₹100 sample fee deta hai aur lot ka sample bheja jata hai. Pasand nahi aaya to deal wahin cancel, koi token nahi.

3

10% token seller ke account mein

Sample pasand aane par hi 10% token advance seller ke account mein jata hai.

4

Live packing aur "Verified by Vajra" seal

Vajra khud maal check karke buyer ke saath live video par packing karta hai aur seal lagata hai. Isi samay seller sauda chithi par sign karta hai.

5

Transporter aur Hold-LR

Parcel buyer ke regular transporter ko diya jata hai. LR consignee seller ke naam par ya hold par banti hai. Buyer apne shehar ke transport office mein call karke parcel verify karta hai.

6

90% payment aur NOC

Buyer baaki 90% seller ke account mein deta hai. Payment aate hi seller NOC release karta hai, jiski copy Vajra buyer ko bhejta hai.

7

Delivery aur claim

Maal pahunchne par buyer unboxing video banaye. Verification report se alag aur agreed limit (3-5%) se zyada issue nikle to seller zimmedar hoga.

Zimmedari kiski?

  • Vajra: verification, live packing, seal aur process ka record. Vajra ek inspection aur facilitation service hai.
  • Seller: maal ki asli quality aur agreed limit se zyada issue ki bharpai (sauda chithi mein likhit).
  • Buyer: verification report padhna, aur claim ke liye seal sahi hone par unboxing video rakhna.

Fee

Vajra ka service fee/commission har deal se pehle likhit mein bataya jata hai. Chhupa hua koi charge nahi.
